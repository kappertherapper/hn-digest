### Astra can read and write weights to improve Qwen
Researchers demonstrate that LLMs like Astra can directly read and edit neural network weights, enabling tasks like fixing reasoning loops in Qwen3.5-0.8B and improving game-playing networks without traditional training. The technique, called Weight-Space Programming, suggests a future where LLMs optimize models autonomously.

*Why it matters:* This breakthrough could revolutionize model optimization and interpretability, reducing reliance on gradient-based training for certain tasks.


### WTF Ubuntu?
Ubuntu has failed to patch a critical Linux kernel vulnerability (CVE-2026-98432, CVSS 9.8) for nearly three weeks, despite working exploits and patches available in Debian. The delay has led users to question Ubuntu's reliability and security promises, with some migrating away.

*Why it matters:* The incident highlights the risks of relying on vendor-patched distributions for critical infrastructure and raises questions about Ubuntu's enterprise-grade claims.

*Why it matters:* The prolonged lack of a patch underscores the criticality of timely security updates and the risks of relying on unpatched systems for production workloads.
