### Google Search is now less than half of Alphabet’s revenue
For the first time, Google Search contributed less than 50% to Alphabet's total revenue in Q3 2026, dropping to 49.7%. Google Cloud revenue grew significantly to 24.1% of the total, while other segments like YouTube ads and subscriptions made up the remaining 26.2%.

*Why it matters:* This shift highlights the growing importance of cloud services and diversified revenue streams for Alphabet, reducing reliance on search advertising.

### The case of the 9 bit AES key
A security analysis revealed that some PDF implementations generate AES-256 encryption keys from a mere 9 bits of entropy, making them trivial to crack. The issue affects multiple PDF software, including MuPDF and Foxit.

*Why it matters:* This highlights critical vulnerabilities in widely used encryption implementations, emphasizing the need for secure random number generation in cryptographic systems.
