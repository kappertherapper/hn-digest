### OpenCode Hacked: User credentials leaked
OpenCode experienced a security breach due to a large-scale distillation attack, impacting 1.4% of its Console users. The company has notified affected users via email and is collaborating with Anthropic and others to address the ongoing incident.

*Why it matters:* This incident highlights the persistent risks of security breaches in cloud-based development platforms, emphasizing the need for robust authentication and rapid incident response.

### Bleached corals thrive within days when touching healthy neighbour

*Why it matters:* Understanding coral recovery mechanisms is critical for conservation efforts, especially as climate change continues to threaten marine biodiversity.
