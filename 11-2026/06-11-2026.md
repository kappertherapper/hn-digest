### Blender's UI and Runtime: Much Faster Than GPUI

*Why it matters:* This could reshape UI development in Blender, a critical tool for 3D modeling and animation, by offering a faster alternative to existing frameworks.


### Dutch 'idealist' bank ASN blocks non-Google Android, including GrapheneOS
