### OpenCode Hacked: User credentials leaked

OpenCode disclosed a security breach impacting 1.4% of its Console users due to a large-scale distillation attack. The company has notified affected users via email and is collaborating with Anthropic and others to address the ongoing incident. Further details remain undisclosed as the investigation continues.

*Why it matters:* This highlights the persistent risks of supply-chain and distillation attacks targeting AI platforms, emphasizing the need for robust security measures in AI infrastructure.

### Bleached corals thrive within days when touching healthy neighbour

A study in Nature reveals that bleached corals can recover within days when in contact with healthy neighboring corals. This rapid recovery suggests potential resilience mechanisms in coral ecosystems. The findings offer hope for coral reef conservation amid climate change pressures.

*Why it matters:* This discovery could inform coral restoration strategies, providing a natural solution to mitigate the impacts of bleaching events.

### Furifont, a Japanese font that shows furigana

*Why it matters:* This tool enhances accessibility for Japanese language learners and improves readability for complex texts, bridging gaps in language education.
