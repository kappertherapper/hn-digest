### Local Wikipedia: Wikipedia, but only for things a half hour walk from you
Local Wikipedia filters Wikipedia entries to show only locations within a 30-minute walk from your current position. The tool uses geocoded Wikipedia pages to create a hyperlocal encyclopedia, limiting results to the closest 25 or 500 entries based on proximity. It emphasizes privacy by keeping location data client-side.

*Why it matters:* This project reimagines how we access local knowledge by making Wikipedia’s vast content immediately relevant to our immediate surroundings.
