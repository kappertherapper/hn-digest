### OpenCode Hacked: User credentials leaked
OpenCode disclosed a security breach impacting 1.4% of its Console users due to a large-scale distillation attack. The company has notified affected users via email but has not shared further details as the incident remains ongoing. Anthropic and other partners are reportedly assisting in the investigation.

*Why it matters:* This breach highlights the persistent risks of credential theft in AI and developer tooling ecosystems, underscoring the need for robust security measures.

### Solar surpasses nuclear power globally for first time
Solar energy has overtaken nuclear power as the world’s leading source of electricity generation. This milestone reflects the rapid scaling of renewable energy infrastructure and declining costs of solar technology. The shift underscores the global transition toward sustainable energy sources.

*Why it matters:* This marks a critical turning point in the energy sector, signaling the accelerating decline of fossil fuels and the rise of renewables.


### Bleached corals thrive within days when touching healthy neighbour
A study published in Nature found that bleached corals can recover within days when in contact with healthy neighboring corals. The research suggests that physical proximity to healthy corals triggers rapid biological recovery mechanisms. This discovery offers hope for coral reef conservation efforts.

*Why it matters:* Understanding coral resilience could provide actionable insights for restoring damaged reefs and mitigating the impacts of climate change on marine ecosystems.


### Furifont, a Japanese font that shows furigana
Furifont is a novel Japanese font that automatically displays furigana (small phonetic guides) alongside kanji characters. The font simplifies reading Japanese text by embedding pronunciation hints directly into the typography. It is designed for educational and accessibility purposes.

*Why it matters:* This innovation could improve literacy and accessibility for learners of Japanese, particularly those struggling with kanji recognition.


### Making chicken transparent using Dorito dust

*Why it matters:* While purely experimental, this quirky approach highlights the intersection of food science and optics, sparking interest in unconventional scientific demonstrations.
