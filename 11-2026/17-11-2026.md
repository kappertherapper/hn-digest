### Many of Amazon’s Black Friday and Cyber Monday “deals” offered no savings
An investigation by Groundwork Collaborative reveals that nearly one-third of Amazon’s Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals offered no actual savings to shoppers. The report highlights how some products were priced higher during these events than at other times.

*Why it matters:* This exposes deceptive pricing practices that mislead consumers during major shopping events.

*Why it matters:* Such outages highlight the fragility of critical financial infrastructure and its impact on everyday transactions.
