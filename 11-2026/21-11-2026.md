### Levent Alpöge claims proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis [pdf]
Levent Alpöge released a 17-million-line Lean proof claiming to verify the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis. The Lean and Mathlib communities have rallied to verify the proof's correctness, with multiple independent checkers confirming its validity. The proof is built directly on Mathlib and compiles without errors.

*Why it matters:* If verified, this would resolve one of mathematics' most famous unsolved problems, with profound implications for number theory and cryptography.
