### Wine ported to OpenBSD
A new port of Wine to OpenBSD has been proposed, enabling the running of 64-bit Windows software on the BSD system. The port, developed collaboratively, supports a wide range of applications, including games like The Witcher 3. Limitations remain, such as the lack of WoW64 support for 32-bit software.

*Why it matters:* This port expands OpenBSD's software compatibility, offering users access to a broader ecosystem of Windows applications without requiring a Windows license.

### Let's Encrypt is adding two additional remote perspectives for domain validation
Let's Encrypt is expanding its domain validation process by adding two more remote perspectives, increasing the total to seven validation requests. This change, required by new baseline security standards, aims to improve resilience against BGP attacks and enhance certificate issuance security.

*Why it matters:* Stronger domain validation mechanisms protect against certificate hijacking and BGP attacks, ensuring the integrity of the web's HTTPS ecosystem.
