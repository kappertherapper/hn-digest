### Local Wikipedia: Wikipedia, but only for things a half hour walk from you
Local Wikipedia filters Wikipedia articles to show only those within a 30-minute walk from your location. It uses geocoding to curate nearby landmarks, businesses, and points of interest, creating a hyper-local encyclopedia. The tool emphasizes privacy by not sharing your location in shared links.

*Why it matters:* It demonstrates how AI and geospatial data can personalize information to a user's immediate surroundings, offering a novel way to explore local knowledge.


### Docker is weird
This post dives into the unconventional aspects of Docker, from its reliance on Linux kernel features like namespaces and cgroups to its Go-based runtime quirks. It highlights how Docker containers are just processes with restrictions and how images are essentially folders. The author also critiques Go's suitability for container runtimes.

*Why it matters:* Understanding Docker's internals can help developers debug issues, optimize performance, and make informed decisions about containerization tools.


### WorldTUI: I trained a 360M model to pretend to be a terminal
WorldTUI is a 360M-parameter language model fine-tuned to simulate a terminal interface. It responds to keypresses and predicts screen changes, mimicking the behavior of real shells and programs like Vim. The model can create files, run commands, and even play games like Snake.

*Why it matters:* It showcases the potential of AI to simulate complex interactive systems, paving the way for more immersive and adaptive user interfaces.

### The foolishness of 'natural language programming' (1978)
This archival piece by Edsger Dijkstra critiques the idea of programming in natural language, arguing that it doesn't eliminate errors but merely shifts the burden of precision. Dijkstra emphasizes the importance of formalism in programming to avoid mistakes. The essay remains relevant in discussions about AI-driven coding assistants.

*Why it matters:* It provides historical context for the ongoing debate about the role of natural language in programming and the importance of formal methods.
